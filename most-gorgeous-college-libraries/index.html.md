# 25 Most Beautiful College Libraries in the World: The Coolest & Prettiest Spaces

College libraries around the world have become major tourist attractions and the ultimate dream destinations for bibliophiles and students alike! While you shouldn't choose a university solely based on its study spaces, finding a campus where you feel inspired and comfortable makes all the difference. If you happen to feel most at home surrounded by thousands of classic volumes on multi-story shelves in the most beautiful college libraries, you're not alone. Colleges with the best libraries offer breathtaking design, serene atmospheres, and historic architecture that make studying a true pleasure. Explore our curated list of the coolest university libraries and prettiest university libraries across the globe!

## 1. Trinity College Library-Ireland

![Trinity College Library-Ireland](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/h5/p7/qqlnpw6b_427x640.jpg)

Via [Trinity College Library (1 of ...](https://www.flickr.com/photos/x1brett/6382651341/)
Ireland's ancient and renowned institution, [Trinity College Library](https://www.tcd.ie/library/), is world-famous for its magnificent Long Room. You could spend an eternity wandering beneath its soaring wooden barrel-vaulted ceiling and find something incredible to read every single day! As one of the best college libraries in Europe, it holds centuries of literary treasures.

- Home to the famous 9th-century illuminated manuscript, the Book of Kells

- Features the iconic 200-foot Long Room lined with marble busts of great philosophers

- Holds over 200,000 of the university's oldest rare books

## 2. University of Washington Library-US

![University of Washington Library-US](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/pg/oj/k2a9uq7e_424x612.jpg)

Via [Suzzallo Library Staircase HDR](https://www.flickr.com/photos/feekner/3606587518/)
Even though the United States is significantly younger than most European nations, it still hosts a great number of beautiful colleges and libraries! The [Suzzallo Library](https://www.lib.washington.edu/) at the University of Washington features grand stained-glass windows and vaulted ceilings that give it a Cathedral-like feel, ranking it among the best university libraries in the us.

## 3. University of Amsterdam Digital Library-the Netherlands

![University of Amsterdam Digital Library-the Netherlands](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/db/up/bwlhmjij_600x704.jpg)

Via [A Library Designed for the ...](https://www.fastcompany.com/1662561/a-library-designed-for-the-post-print-era)
For a more modern, minimalist library, check out this sleek digital library space at the University of Amsterdam! Designed specifically for the post-print era, it stands out among coolest college libraries by offering bright, open study alcoves tailored for modern research and digital collaboration.

## 4. University of Michigan Law Library-US

![University of Michigan Law Library-US](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/cp/sv/kt5jsew1_600x782.jpg)

Via [Michigan Law Library](https://www.flickr.com/photos/trenton_b/6479549887/)
If you like the cozy feeling of studying in an old-fashioned library, you'll love Michigan Law's library! With its soaring ceiling, intricate woodwork, and majestic stained glass windows, this iconic space is regularly cited when ranking colleges with the prettiest libraries.

## 5. Queen's College Library, University of Oxford-England

![Queen's College Library, University of Oxford-England](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/j2/i8/w5ctlejk_600x917.jpg)

Via [Upper Library - Queens College ...](https://www.flickr.com/photos/stevecadman/232497672/)
It's no secret that Oxford University is gorgeous, so it shouldn't come as a surprise that Queen's College Library is equally stunning! Featuring classical 17th-century architecture and elaborate plasterwork, it remains a premier destination for anyone looking for beautiful college library settings in the UK.

## 6. Widener Library, Harvard-US

_ Via Klub Buku Indonesia on Twitter_
Harvard is one of the most well-known universities in the world, so it's only right that they have a impressive library to match! The flagship [Widener Library](https://library.harvard.edu/libraries/widener) houses tens of miles of bookshelves and is a staple among the best college libraries in the us.

## 7. Cornell University Library-US

_ Via The Gifts Of Life_
Cornell is another Ivy League university with a stunning library! Often referred to by students as the cornell hogwarts library due to its ornate wrought-iron decorative balconies and multi-tiered book stacks, the A.D. White Library inside [Cornell University Library](https://www.library.cornell.edu/) is truly magical.

## 8. George Peabody Library, Johns Hopkins University-US

![George Peabody Library, Johns Hopkins University-US](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ai/ay/rax02qtv_558x510.jpg)

Via [Community Post: The Ten Most ...](https://www.buzzfeed.com/booklover84/10-most-beautiful-school-libraries-76ku)
Known as the "cathedral of books," the George Peabody Library at Johns Hopkins University features six tiers of cast-iron balconies looking over a central atrium. It's easily one of the most beautiful university libraries in the United States and a favorite venue for special events.

## 9. General Library, the University of Coimbra-Portugal

![General Library, the University of Coimbra-Portugal](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ia/y2/gdiynv68_600x474.jpg)

Via [flavorwire.com](https://www.flavorwire.com/240819/the-25-most-beautiful-college-libraries-in-the-world)
In Portugal you'll find one of the most captivating libraries of all at the University of Coimbra! The Joanina Library section features opulent Baroque decoration, gilded archways, and exotic hardwood tables, making it a standout entry among top college libraries worldwide.

## 10. Lehigh University Library-US

![Lehigh University Library-US](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/zm/x8/m91qh9yn_600x900.jpg)

Via [Readers' Choice: 20 More Beautiful ...](https://www.flavorwire.com/244356/readers-choice-20-more-beautiful-college-libraries-from-around-the-world)
Lehigh University is beautifully constructed all around, but the library especially speaks volumes! Famous for its rotunda architecture and soaring stained-glass dome, Linderman Library is constantly featured in popular linderman library photos and student study guides.

- Stunning Victorian Gothic architecture with a circular skylight ceiling

- Comprehensive collection of rare books and historical manuscripts

- Quiet study alcoves surrounding a grand central reading room

## 11. Maughan Library, King's College-England

_ Via GuardianWitness - Round Reading Room ..._
This circular library will have your mind going in circles as you try to decide where to explore first! Located in London, the maughan library at King's College features a magnificent dodecagonal Round Reading Room that inspired scenes in major films and attracts scholars from around the globe.

## 12. Copenhagen University Library-Denmark

_ Via Copenhagen University Library_
Denmark is rumored to have the happiest citizens on the planet, and with access to libraries like this one, it's no wonder why! Bright, airy, and steeped in Scandinavian design, it ranks high among colleges with pretty libraries.

## 13. St. John’s College Old Library, Cambridge University-England

_ Via Blue Pueblo_
One of England's most prestigious universities, Cambridge, hosts a stunning library called St. John's! Built in the early 17th century, its historic timber bookcases and dark oak panelling make it a favorite for students seeking quiet, traditional study spaces.

## 14. Slocum Hall, Wesleyan University-US

![Slocum Hall, Wesleyan University-US](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/dp/r7/zynvontu_462x900.jpg)

Via [Slocum Hall Romanesque Arcade And ...](https://fineartamerica.com/featured/slocum-hall-romanesque-arcade-and-stained-glass-skylight-ohio-wesleyan-university-brian-mollenkopf.html)
Beneath the breathtaking stained glass windows of this college lies an even more breathtaking wealth of books worth reading! Featuring Romanesque arcades and ceiling skylights, Slocum Hall offers an unforgettable atmosphere for learning.

## 15. Sterling Memorial Library, Yale University-US

![Sterling Memorial Library, Yale University-US](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/j5/rs/ehnh8odm_450x577.jpg)

Via [Yale Political Union Colloquium on ...](http://ypukentstate.blogspot.com/)
One of Yale's most popular sites on campus is the Sterling Memorial Library, and it's no mystery why! Built in the Cathedral Gothic style, it features over 3,000 stained glass windows and stands as one of the best university libraries in North America.

## 16. Deakin University Library-Australia

_ Via feedproxy.google.com_
Down under they have stellar college libraries, too! Like the Deakin University Library, at which the walls are lined with books and contemporary study nooks designed for interactive learning and student comfort.

## 17. The Harper Library Reading Room, Univ. of Chicago-US

![The Harper Library Reading Room, Univ. of Chicago-US](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/nr/ne/zgokcl0w_600x399.jpg)

Via [Community Post: The Ten Most ...](https://www.buzzfeed.com/booklover84/10-most-beautiful-school-libraries-76ku)
Reading in this library would be nearly impossible, with all the beautiful architecture there to distract you! The Harper Memorial Library Reading Room features soaring vaulted ceilings and stone carvings inspired by Europe's great cathedrals.

## 18. Gonville & Caius Library, Cambridge University-England

![Gonville & Caius Library, Cambridge University-England](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/mf/zm/hxf2mbta_480x640.jpg)

Via [gonville & caius library, cambridge](https://www.flickr.com/photos/idlethink/614011807/)
That's right, Cambridge made the list twice! The school has many libraries, all of which are uniquely beautiful, much like [Trinity College Library](#1) in Dublin.

## 19. Thomas Fisher Rare Book Library, University of Toronto-Canada

![Thomas Fisher Rare Book Library, University of Toronto-Canada](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ny/o8/k6ulow1h_600x600.jpg)

Via [49 Breathtaking Libraries From All ...](https://www.buzzfeed.com/mattortile/49-breathtaking-libraries-from-all-over-the-world?sub=2432158_1377674)
If you've planned any college tours in Canada, you certainly ought to visit the Rare Book Library at the University of Toronto. Holding the largest repository of publicly accessible rare books in Canada, its multi-level concrete and glass mezzanine design is truly striking.

## 20. Vilnius University Library-Lithuania

![Vilnius University Library-Lithuania](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fl/ff/jxnetnk6_468x640.jpg)

Via [Ateik ir nufotografuok VU biblioteką-70](https://www.flickr.com/photos/49437499@N07/4881326815)
Although this library appears small, it still deserves ample appreciation for the opulent detail and elegant structure. Founded in 1570, Vilnius University Library is one of the oldest and nicest college libraries in Eastern Europe.

## 21. Riggs Library, Georgetown University-US

![Riggs Library, Georgetown University-US](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/h3/xi/y5v1051p_480x640.jpg)

Via [P2240081](https://www.flickr.com/photos/70696553@N00/103959665/)
Georgetown itself is a gorgeous college which looks almost like a castle! The libraries inside are no different—especially riggs library, one of the few surviving 19th-century cast-iron libraries in the country.

## 22. Geisel Library, UC San Diego-US

![Geisel Library, UC San Diego-US](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/d1/bw/o5wycbbq_600x600.jpg)

Via [Geisel Library](https://www.flickr.com/photos/88017382@N00/4533276057/)
University of California San Diego is in a gorgeous area and has an overall gorgeous campus, but their [Geisel Library](#22) is certainly the crown jewel! Named in honor of Theodor Seuss Geisel (Dr. Seuss), its futuristic brutalist architecture makes it one of the coolest university libraries anywhere.

## 23. Armstrong Browning Library at Baylor University-US

![Armstrong Browning Library at Baylor University-US](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ua/m9/honowfal_600x457.jpg)

Via [Librophiliac Love Letter: United States ...](https://www.atlasobscura.com/articles/librophiliac-love-letter-part-1-the-usa)
The stained glass windows at this college library pour a gorgeous light onto your favorite novels! Housing the world's largest collection of English poets Robert and Elizabeth Barrett Browning memorabilia, it's a peaceful sanctuary for literary lovers.

## 24. Tama Art University Library-Japan

![Tama Art University Library-Japan](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/kf/fj/rolemnpr_450x607.jpg)

Via [simplypi](https://blog.vanstee.be/post/7923734155)
In Tokyo you'll find one of the most unconventional yet gorgeous college libraries in the world! Designed by Toyo Ito, its sweeping concrete arches and glass walls make it a masterpiece of modern architecture.

## 25. The Library of the Faculty of Philology, the Free University of Berlin-Germany

![The Library of the Faculty of Philology, the Free University of Berlin-Germany](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/o4/c4/llrubvft_600x593.jpg)

Via [Berlin Architecture](https://www.flickr.com/photos/svenwerk/sets/1053588/)
Imagine studying at this German university, which is arguably the most modern and creative of all! Designed by Norman Foster and known colloquially as "The Berlin Brain" due to its dome shape, it represents futuristic design at its best.

Across the globe there are countless college libraries that we’d all love to visit one day! Which of these libraries are at the top of your must-see list?
